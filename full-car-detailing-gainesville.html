FIND THE RIGHT FIT

Is Full Detail
right for your car?

Choose Full Detail when both the cabin and exterior need extra attention. It brings deep interior work, paint decontamination, and exterior protection into one appointment.

One visit. The complete package.

The interior receives the deeper cleaning and material care of Interior Focused. Outside, we wash, decontaminate, clay, and protect the finish with the treatments included in Exterior Focused. The six-month paint sealant is included in the published package price.

BEFORE YOUR DETAIL

Your questions,
answered.

Can I upgrade the paint protection? ＋

Full Detail includes a six-month paint sealant. Published upgrades are one-year protection for an additional $30 or a two-year Ceramic Graphene Coating for an additional $120. Mention your preference in the form.

How long does a full detail take? ＋

Plan for a minimum of six hours. We will confirm the timing and appointment details by text after reviewing your request.

What does it cost for my vehicle? ＋

Full Detail is $400 for a sedan, $470 for an SUV, or $520 for a truck or large SUV. Tell us the vehicle type and any specific requests so we can confirm the details.
