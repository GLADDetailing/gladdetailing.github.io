BEFORE YOU BELONG

A few details,
made clear.

Are these prices per visit? +

No. All listed membership prices are monthly costs. Choose monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly visits; your monthly price depends on your plan and vehicle size.

Am I a member as soon as I apply? +

Your application starts the conversation. We review it and contact you to confirm your vehicle’s maintenance needs, plan, recurring schedule, and rate before membership begins.

Can visits fit my personal schedule? +

Members receive priority when arranging recurring visits. Share your preferred days and times in the application. We’ll work with you and confirm availability before setting a schedule.

When do I receive the complimentary clay & seal? +

Your first complimentary clay & seal comes after six months of service. After that, the reward is included once every year.

How does grandfathered pricing work? +

Your membership rate stays locked while your membership remains continuously active. We confirm your starting rate with you before membership begins.

Are the community group and partner discounts available now? +

Those benefits are coming soon. We’ll announce the community group, club events, and discount codes with future Gainesville partners as they become available.
